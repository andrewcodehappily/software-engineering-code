# SEC Case 001: People v. Restart

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

> Fictional illustrative precedent, not an actual GitHub case or legal judgment.

## Facts

A noncritical UI defect vanished after restarting. A developer claimed that the root cause had been permanently eliminated.

## Reasoning

The court distinguishes mitigation from proof of repair under Article 746. Restarting is lawful; claiming certainty without evidence is not supported by the record.

## Disposition

The claim of permanent repair is dismissed without prejudice. New evidence may reopen the case.
