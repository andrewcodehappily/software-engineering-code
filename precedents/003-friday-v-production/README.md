# SEC Case 003: Friday v. Production

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

> Fictional illustrative precedent, not an actual GitHub case or legal judgment.

## Facts

A team proposed a cosmetic update shortly before the end of Friday. The justification was that the change contained only two lines.

## Reasoning

Article 10002 concerns deployment risk, not line count. Two lines may have consequences exceeding their length.

## Disposition

The deployment is deferred. The weekend is released from custody.
