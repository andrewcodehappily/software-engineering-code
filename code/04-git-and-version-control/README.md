# Part 4 — Git and Version Control

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

### Article 409 — Merge Conflicts

A merge conflict shall not establish fault on either side; both parties may nevertheless privately condemn the other.

### Article 8192 — Finality of Commits

A commit named `final` shall have no binding effect as a final version. The same shall apply to `final-final`.

### Article 8193 — Right Against Self-Incrimination

An engineer who discovers their own name through `git blame` shall have the right to remain silent.

### Article 8194 — Commit Attribution

The author of the last commit shall not automatically be deemed responsible for every defect in the repository.

### Article 8195 — Stash Custody

A stash abandoned for more than one year shall be presumed to contain either irreplaceable work or absolutely nothing of value; opening it shall be required to establish which.
