<a name="article-24001"></a>

### Article 24001 — Standing

A user affected by a reproducible defect may submit an issue without first obtaining a law degree or a compiler license.

<a name="article-24002"></a>

### Article 24002 — Burden of proof

A party asserting that a change improves performance shall provide appropriate measurements rather than additional exclamation marks.

<a name="article-24003"></a>

### Article 24003 — Admissible screenshots

A screenshot may establish visible behavior but shall not alone establish the internal cause of that behavior.

<a name="article-24004"></a>

### Article 24004 — Minimal reproduction

A minimal reproduction shall omit unrelated complexity without removing the facts necessary to reproduce the alleged defect.

<a name="article-24005"></a>

### Article 24005 — Witness logs

Logs from different machines shall be correlated using relevant identifiers and time information before their chronology is accepted.

<a name="article-24006"></a>

### Article 24006 — Conflicting testimony

Where two engineers observe different results, their environments shall be examined before either is accused of lying.

<a name="article-24007"></a>

### Article 24007 — Appeals

A closed issue may be reopened upon material new evidence rather than merely a renewed desire to win the argument.

<a name="article-24008"></a>

### Article 24008 — Recusal

An author reviewing their own disputed change may provide evidence but should seek independent review where available.

<a name="article-24009"></a>

### Article 24009 — Discovery

A request for evidence shall be limited to relevant and appropriately shareable information, not the entire contents of a developer's computer.

<a name="article-24010"></a>

### Article 24010 — Settlement

An issue may be resolved by correcting documentation if the dispute concerns the documented contract rather than a software defect.

<a name="article-24011"></a>

### Article 24011 — Default judgment

Silence on an issue shall not be deemed consent to every proposed change.

<a name="article-24012"></a>

### Article 24012 — Final judgment

A merged fix shall conclude the particular proceeding without immunizing future versions from new claims.
