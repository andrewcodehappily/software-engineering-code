<a name="article-18001"></a>

### Article 18001 — Example Validity

An example in the documentation shall be considered a public promise subject to reasonable testing.

<a name="article-18002"></a>

### Article 18002 — Stale Instructions

Instructions referring to a button removed three versions ago shall be eligible for expedited correction.

<a name="article-18003"></a>

### Article 18003 — Comment Motive

A comment explaining why a decision was made shall not be deemed redundant merely because the implementation is visible.

<a name="article-18004"></a>

### Article 18004 — TODO Limitation

A TODO bearing no owner or date may remain pending until the original author has forgotten its existence.

<a name="article-18005"></a>

### Article 18005 — Readme Jurisdiction

A README shall direct readers to the relevant document before requiring them to read the entire repository.

<a name="article-18006"></a>

### Article 18006 — Setup Reproducibility

A setup guide shall not presume possession of files that exist only on the author's computer.

<a name="article-18007"></a>

### Article 18007 — Glossary Authority

Established technical terms may retain their English spelling where translation would cause confusion.

<a name="article-18008"></a>

### Article 18008 — Diagram Accuracy

An architecture diagram shall not be declared current merely because its colors have been updated.

<a name="article-18009"></a>

### Article 18009 — Changelog Scope

A changelog entry stating miscellaneous improvements shall not preclude readers from requesting particulars.

<a name="article-18010"></a>

### Article 18010 — Document Inheritance

A copied document shall not inherit the truth of its source after the systems described therein diverge.
