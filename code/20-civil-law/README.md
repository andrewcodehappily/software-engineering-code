<a name="article-23001"></a>

### Article 23001 — Interface obligations

A published interface shall be interpreted according to its documented contract rather than the private intentions of its author.

<a name="article-23002"></a>

### Article 23002 — Ownership of TODOs

A TODO without an owner may be assigned through agreement, but shall not automatically descend to the newest employee.

<a name="article-23003"></a>

### Article 23003 — Borrowed snippets

A borrowed snippet shall retain applicable attribution obligations even when its variable names have been changed.

<a name="article-23004"></a>

### Article 23004 — Shared modules

A shared module shall not be altered as if it had only one consumer merely because its maintainer has only one monitor.

<a name="article-23005"></a>

### Article 23005 — Deprecation notice

A deprecation shall afford users a reasonable opportunity to migrate where the published compatibility commitment requires it.

<a name="article-23006"></a>

### Article 23006 — Delivery acceptance

Acceptance shall be assessed against agreed criteria rather than a newly discovered preference for a different shade of blue.

<a name="article-23007"></a>

### Article 23007 — Compensation in tests

Where a defect exposes a missing test, the parties may settle the engineering dispute by adding an appropriate regression test.

<a name="article-23008"></a>

### Article 23008 — Maintenance boundaries

A volunteer's past contribution shall not create an indefinite obligation to maintain every derivative work.

<a name="article-23009"></a>

### Article 23009 — API succession

A replacement service shall not inherit the trust of its predecessor until relevant compatibility and security conditions are established.

<a name="article-23010"></a>

### Article 23010 — Dispute settlement

Two competing implementations may coexist temporarily during evaluation without either author being declared the losing party.

<a name="article-23011"></a>

### Article 23011 — Technical debt maturity

A debt deferred to the next sprint shall be visible in the next sprint's planning record rather than silently renewed forever.

<a name="article-23012"></a>

### Article 23012 — Reasonable reliance

A consumer relying on documented behavior shall not be accused of misuse merely because a maintainer has since regretted the design.
