# Part 2 — Bug Administration

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

### Article 404 — Missing Bugs

Failure to locate a bug shall not constitute evidence of its nonexistence.

### Article 500 — Mutual Right of Suspicion

Pending investigation, the frontend and backend teams shall each retain the right to reasonably suspect the other.

### Article 743, Section 928 — Non-Reproducible Bug Immunity

If a bug cannot be consistently reproduced and all existing functionality remains operational, further modifications shall be prohibited, lest they introduce additional, consistently reproducible bugs.

### Article 744 — Bug Reproduction

Bugs introduced in the course of repairing a bug shall not be classified as supplementary deliverables.

### Article 745 — Observer Effect

A bug that disappears upon recording, screenshotting, or demonstration shall be classified as a quantum bug, and proceedings shall be suspended.

### Article 746 — Restart Amnesty

A noncritical error disappearing after a restart may be provisionally dismissed, subject to the possibility of recidivism.

### Article 747 — Diagnostic Logging

Where a defect disappears upon the addition of a log statement, said statement may be admitted as evidence but shall not constitute proof of repair.

### Article 748 — Duplicate Complaints

The closure of a duplicate report shall not reduce the number of affected users.
