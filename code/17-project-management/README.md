<a name="article-20001"></a>

### Article 20001 — Estimate Conditions

An estimate shall remain conditional on the requirements on which it was based.

<a name="article-20002"></a>

### Article 20002 — Scope Creep

A succession of minor requests shall not be presumed to constitute a minor aggregate change.

<a name="article-20003"></a>

### Article 20003 — Deadline Arithmetic

Moving a deadline earlier shall not, by itself, reduce the amount of work remaining.

<a name="article-20004"></a>

### Article 20004 — Status Color

A project marked green shall not thereby become exempt from its unresolved blockers.

<a name="article-20005"></a>

### Article 20005 — Meeting Output

The duration of a meeting shall not constitute evidence that a corresponding quantity of decisions was made.

<a name="article-20006"></a>

### Article 20006 — Task Dependencies

A task blocked by another task shall not be accelerated solely by changing its priority label.

<a name="article-20007"></a>

### Article 20007 — Definition Changes

Changing the definition of completion after delivery shall constitute a new requirement, not retroactive incompletion.

<a name="article-20008"></a>

### Article 20008 — Demo Exception

A successful demonstration shall not automatically establish the readiness of every path not demonstrated.

<a name="article-20009"></a>

### Article 20009 — Priority Inflation

Where every task is designated the highest priority, the designation shall cease to distinguish any task.

<a name="article-20010"></a>

### Article 20010 — Project Resurrection

A project declared permanently cancelled may be revived, provided that its previous cancellation is not retroactively renamed a planned pause.
