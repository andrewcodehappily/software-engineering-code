### Article 9527, Section 3 — Legislative Growth

The length of this Code shall grow in proportion to technical debt. Lack of readership shall not impede legislative expansion.

### Article 9527, Section 4 — Selective Citation

An engineer lacking time to read this Code may directly cite any provision favorable to their position.

### Article 9527, Section 5 — Legislative Relief

Where no favorable provision exists, an engineer may petition for legislative relief by submitting a pull request.

### Article 9527, Section 6 — Prohibition of Legislative Padding

No provision shall be duplicated solely to satisfy the target line count. Such duplication shall be deemed legislative technical debt.

### Article 2147483647 — Residual Jurisdiction

Matters not governed by this Code shall be resolved by reference to Stack Overflow, official documentation, GitHub Issues, and the survival instincts of engineers.

### Article 2147483648 — Integer Overflow

This article shall not enter into force on systems using signed 32-bit integers.
