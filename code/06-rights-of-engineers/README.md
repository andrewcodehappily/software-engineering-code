# Part 6 — Rights of Engineers

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

### Article 65535 — Time Estimation Immunity

The expression “five minutes” shall not constitute a legally binding estimate of completion time.

### Article 65536 — Scope of Small Requests

The effort required by any request beginning with “just one small feature” shall not be assessed by its word count alone.

### Article 65537 — Right to Documentation

An engineer assigned to maintain an undocumented system may request documentation, including documentation explaining why no documentation exists.

### Article 65538 — Meeting Conversion

A meeting whose sole outcome is a written status update may be converted into a written status update without prejudice.
