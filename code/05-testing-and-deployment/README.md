# Part 5 — Testing and Deployment

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

### Article 10001 — Effect of Passing Tests

The passing of all tests shall establish only that all tests actually written have passed.

### Article 10002 — Friday Deployment Restrictions

Except in a major emergency, substantial deployments shall not occur shortly before the close of business on Friday. Violators shall be deemed to have voluntarily waived their weekend.

### Article 10003 — Unpredictability of Users

Engineers shall not presume that users will operate software as intended, even where their actual manner of operation defies ordinary human logic.

### Article 10004 — Test Environment Independence

Success in the staging environment shall not compel production to reach the same judgment.

### Article 10005 — Flaky Test Testimony

A test that alternates between passing and failing without a change to the code shall be considered an unreliable witness pending investigation.
