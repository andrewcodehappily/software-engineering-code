# Executive Orders

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

This is a fictional implementing instrument for entertainment, not actual engineering policy.

## Order EO-001: Friday ceasefire

Nonessential deployments near the end of Friday shall be deferred in accordance with Article 10002. The weekend is not a staging environment.

## Order EO-002: Historical preservation

Code protected under Article 1024 shall be documented before alteration. Archaeological excavation shall preferably occur on a separate branch.

## Order EO-003: Translation amnesty

English-only contributions may proceed under the contribution policy without requiring their authors to acquire a second language.

## Order EO-004: Meeting economy

A meeting converted to an asynchronous written update under Article 65538 shall not be replaced by a meeting to discuss the conversion.

## Order EO-005: Evidence before celebration

Success announcements concerning tool operations shall follow verification under Article 13009, not precede it.
