# Bug Investigation Regulations

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

This is a fictional implementing instrument for entertainment, not actual engineering policy.

## Section 1: Intake

A report shall identify the observed behavior, expected behavior and available reproduction steps. The phrase “it exploded” may initiate proceedings but shall not conclude them.

## Section 2: Evidence preservation

Logs and screenshots shall be preserved where useful, with secrets and personal information removed before publication.

## Section 3: Quantum classification

A bug disappearing during demonstration may be classified under Article 745, provided its prior existence is not thereby denied.

## Section 4: Suspension

Article 743, Section 928 permits suspension of speculative modifications to noncritical systems. Reopening shall be permitted upon new evidence.

## Section 5: Emergency exception

Security, safety and data integrity incidents shall remain subject to actual incident procedures regardless of reproducibility.

## Section 6: Restart verdict

A successful restart shall be recorded as mitigation rather than proof of a permanent repair.
