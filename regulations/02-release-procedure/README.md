# Release and Deployment Regulations

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [Section index](../README.md) · [Master index](../../README.md)

This is a fictional implementing instrument for entertainment, not actual engineering policy.

## Section 1: Release eligibility

A release candidate shall not become stable solely through deletion of the word candidate.

## Section 2: Friday exception

An emergency Friday deployment shall identify the emergency and the person responsible for monitoring its consequences.

## Section 3: Rollback preparation

The rollback procedure shall be available before it becomes the most urgently requested document in the organization.

## Section 4: Release notices

Release notes shall distinguish completed changes from aspirations, wishes and items accidentally left on the roadmap.

## Section 5: Monitoring

The absence of complaints during the first minute shall not constitute a declaration of victory.

## Section 6: Rollback dignity

A justified rollback shall not be treated as a crime against the reputation of its author.
