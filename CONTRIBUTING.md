# Legislative Procedure

[繁體中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh-TW.md)

## Proposing legislation

1. Open a proposed amendment or legal dispute issue, or submit a pull request directly.
2. Identify whether the proposal amends the Constitution or the Code. Select a unique article or section number within that instrument. Existing numbers must not be reassigned.
3. Add or amend the provision in the appropriate chapter or part directory. English-only contributions are fully welcome.
4. Use formal legal language for absurd but recognizable engineering situations.
5. Explain the legislative intent and, where available, an anonymized engineering incident. Do not disclose private data or secrets.
6. Submit the pull request for judicial review. Ordinary legislation must conform to the Constitution; constitutional amendments require approval from a maintainer with merge authority.

## Language and translation policy

- English is the reference text for legislative review. Contributors are not required to know or write Traditional Chinese.
- A Traditional Chinese translation is welcome but entirely optional. Translation-only pull requests and corrections are welcome as well.
- Maintainers may merge an English-only amendment after ordinary review. A missing translation is not grounds for rejecting or delaying an otherwise acceptable contribution.
- Maintainers or volunteer translators may supply the Traditional Chinese text in the same pull request or a follow-up amendment. If translation is deferred, maintainers shall record the missing or outdated translation in a follow-up issue, including the affected article identifiers and source pull request.
- For a new part or chapter, maintainers shall ensure its Chinese index has a corresponding entry or an English fallback marked as awaiting translation. Existing translations must not silently be presented as current when their source text has changed.
- Translations should preserve meaning, article identifiers, legal tone, and familiar English technical terminology (such as `Fork`, `Commit`, and `API`). Do not invent additional obligations in translation.
- If the versions disagree, the English text governs within this fictional jurisdiction until the discrepancy is corrected. A translation correction need not reopen the underlying legislative decision unless it changes its substance.

## General provisions

Article identifiers remain stable even when files move. New subject directories must be discoverable through the relevant indexes.

The project may remain in alpha indefinitely. Real safety and security guidance takes precedence. Keep humor directed at engineering situations, not individuals.

You are here to propose legislation, not to pass a language proficiency examination.
