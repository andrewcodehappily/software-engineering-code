# The Software Engineering Code

[繁體中文](CODE.zh-TW.md) · [Home](README.md)

Version: v0.0.2-alpha

Enacted under the [Constitution of Software Engineering](CONSTITUTION.md). In this fictional jurisdiction, the Constitution shall prevail in case of conflict.

Purpose: to safeguard the operation of software, the sanity of engineers, and the prevention of repairs becoming incidents in their own right.

This Code is satire, not law or engineering policy. Safety, security, data integrity, and serious incidents require real procedures.

## Part 1 — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Protection of Operational Code

No functioning program shall be modified without sufficient cause.

### Article 2 — Local Environment Defense

The statement “It works on my machine” shall be admissible as a preliminary defense, but shall not constitute grounds for dismissal.

### Article 3 — Presumption of Functionality

Undocumented behavior may provisionally be classified as an unreleased feature until material harm occurs.

### Article 4 — Permanent Temporary Solutions

A temporary solution remaining in service for more than one year shall acquire the status of permanent infrastructure.

## Part 2 — Bug Administration

### Article 404 — Missing Bugs

Failure to locate a bug shall not constitute evidence of its nonexistence.

### Article 500 — Mutual Right of Suspicion

Pending investigation, the frontend and backend teams shall each retain the right to reasonably suspect the other.

### Article 743, Section 928 — Non-Reproducible Bug Immunity

If a bug cannot be consistently reproduced and all existing functionality remains operational, further modifications shall be prohibited, lest they introduce additional, consistently reproducible bugs.

### Article 744 — Bug Reproduction

Bugs introduced in the course of repairing a bug shall not be classified as supplementary deliverables.

### Article 745 — Observer Effect

A bug that disappears upon recording, screenshotting, or demonstration shall be classified as a quantum bug, and proceedings shall be suspended.

### Article 746 — Restart Amnesty

A noncritical error disappearing after a restart may be provisionally dismissed, subject to the possibility of recidivism.

## Part 3 — Code and Technical Debt

### Article 1024 — Protection of Historical Code

Unclaimed code whose purpose is unknown but whose removal causes system failure shall be designated a protected historical monument.

### Article 1025 — Authority of Comments

Where comments conflict with executable behavior, the latter shall prevail.

### Article 2048 — Technical Debt Extensions

Technical debt may be deferred on the grounds that it will be addressed in the next release. No limit shall apply to such extensions.

### Article 4096 — Proportionality in Refactoring

The removal of three lines of code shall not, by itself, justify the introduction of four classes, six files, and two external dependencies.

## Part 4 — Git and Version Control

### Article 409 — Merge Conflicts

A merge conflict shall not establish fault on either side; both parties may nevertheless privately condemn the other.

### Article 8192 — Finality of Commits

A commit named `final` shall have no binding effect as a final version. The same shall apply to `final-final`.

### Article 8193 — Right Against Self-Incrimination

An engineer who discovers their own name through `git blame` shall have the right to remain silent.

## Part 5 — Testing and Deployment

### Article 10001 — Effect of Passing Tests

The passing of all tests shall establish only that all tests actually written have passed.

### Article 10002 — Friday Deployment Restrictions

Except in a major emergency, substantial deployments shall not occur shortly before the close of business on Friday. Violators shall be deemed to have voluntarily waived their weekend.

### Article 10003 — Unpredictability of Users

Engineers shall not presume that users will operate software as intended, even where their actual manner of operation defies ordinary human logic.

## Part 6 — Rights of Engineers

### Article 65535 — Time Estimation Immunity

The expression “five minutes” shall not constitute a legally binding estimate of completion time.

### Article 65536 — Scope of Small Requests

The effort required by any request beginning with “just one small feature” shall not be assessed by its word count alone.

## Part 7 — Supplementary Provisions

### Article 9527, Section 3 — Legislative Growth

The length of this Code shall grow in proportion to technical debt. Lack of readership shall not impede legislative expansion.

### Article 9527, Section 4 — Selective Citation

An engineer lacking time to read this Code may directly cite any provision favorable to their position.

### Article 9527, Section 5 — Legislative Relief

Where no favorable provision exists, an engineer may petition for legislative relief by submitting a pull request.

### Article 2147483647 — Residual Jurisdiction

Matters not governed by this Code shall be resolved by reference to Stack Overflow, official documentation, GitHub Issues, and the survival instincts of engineers.

### Article 2147483648 — Integer Overflow

This article shall not enter into force on systems using signed 32-bit integers.
